# Website V2 Master Brief

## Public identity

The public name is:

# Codie Marillier

The website must avoid placing Codie into one narrow hero label such as “investor,” “entrepreneur” or “builder.”

These are all relevant parts of his life, but none is the whole identity.

## Why the website exists

> This website exists because it is a showcase of who I am personally, and something to hold me accountable for what I want to achieve in the future.

A polished public version is:

> I built this website to document who I am, what I am learning and what I am working towards. It is both a public introduction and a form of accountability—a place where my future actions can be measured against the ambitions I have set for myself.

## The strategic change

The current website primarily answers:

> How does Codie invest?

Website V2 should answer:

> Who is Codie Marillier?

Investing remains a major section, but becomes one chapter within a larger personal website.

## Main purpose

Website V2 should function as:

1. a digital biography;
2. a professional portfolio;
3. a living record of work and projects;
4. a public accountability system;
5. a gateway into Codie’s investing, books, writing, travel and ideas.

## Audience

The main audiences are:

- friends and people who already know Codie;
- potential employers;
- experienced entrepreneurs and mentors;
- future business partners;
- people interested in property, investing, Zimbabwe or Africa;
- future clients or investors, subject to legal and regulatory boundaries.

## Desired first impression

Visitors should initially think:

- ambitious;
- intelligent;
- professional;
- modern;
- technically capable;
- interesting;
- grounded in real-world experience.

## Desired deeper impression

As visitors explore, they should discover that Codie is also:

- funny;
- adventurous;
- honest about mistakes;
- highly curious;
- family-oriented;
- practical;
- capable of hard physical work;
- still figuring out his exact direction.

## Core narrative

Codie is a young Zimbabwean building experience across property, investing, entrepreneurship, practical work, travel, sport and reading.

He has grown up around a family property company, travelled widely, worked in several countries and industries, started projects, made mistakes in financial markets and chosen to document his development publicly.

He is not presenting himself as an established expert.

The compelling story is that he is at the beginning and is building deliberately.

## Five required outcomes

Website V2 must:

1. present Codie as a complete person rather than only an investor;
2. preserve and improve the existing investing, writing and books systems;
3. feel modern, technically sophisticated and memorable;
4. communicate ambition without sounding arrogant or manufactured;
5. remain easy to update as Codie’s life changes.

## Core sections

- Home
- About
- Projects
- Work
- Writing
- Investing
- Books
- Travel
- Timeline
- Now
- Contact

The final navigation may combine some of these where needed.

## Existing brand

**Codie Capital Research** should remain as the name of the investing section or investing publication.

Example:

> Codie Capital Research — my public investment record, research, portfolio reviews and evolving process.

It must no longer be the primary homepage identity.

## Success test

The site succeeds when:

- a friend recognises the real Codie;
- an employer sees initiative and judgement;
- an entrepreneur sees someone worth meeting;
- a visitor understands Zimbabwe’s role in Codie’s life;
- the investing archive remains credible;
- the site feels custom-built rather than templated;
- Codie feels accountable to keep updating it.
