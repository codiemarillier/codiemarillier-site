# Privacy, Accuracy and Publication Rules

## Principle

> Tell Codie’s story fully without treating other people’s lives as content.

## Do not publish without permission

- employer names;
- estate owner names;
- names of employers’ children or family members;
- exact residential locations;
- private conversations;
- confidential company information;
- private financial information;
- unapproved photographs of other people;
- exact contact information not approved for public use.

## Public facts that require verification

- current operating status of O and C Cotswolds Trailers Ltd (legal name confirmed 13 July 2026);
- exact public name of Marillier Properties;
- Inco Freight company styling and role;
- event name in Sweden;
- token name and blockchain;
- token peak market-cap evidence;
- qualification names and dates;
- exact travel-country count;
- exact work dates;
- exact school dates;
- exact portfolio figures;
- exact investment starting date.

## Finance rules

The website must not:

- provide personalised financial advice;
- imply Codie is authorised to manage money;
- present research as a recommendation;
- guarantee returns;
- invite investment into an unapproved structure;
- call a token market cap revenue or profit;
- hide relevant risk disclosures;
- alter performance figures without evidence.

## Zimbabwe property concepts

Describe these as:

- research;
- ideas;
- possible models;
- problems worth exploring.

Do not describe them as:

- available investments;
- guaranteed opportunities;
- regulated products;
- final legal structures.

## University

The website may state that Codie left university after two years.

Use calm, factual language.

Do not frame it as an attack on the institution.

## Failure and mistakes

Mistakes can be discussed where they demonstrate learning.

Recommended structure:

1. what happened;
2. what Codie believed;
3. what went wrong;
4. consequences;
5. what changed;
6. what remains uncertain.

Do not exaggerate or make unsupported claims.

## Political and public figures

If influential public figures are mentioned, make clear that studying selected traits does not imply endorsing every action or belief.

## Photographs

For every image, confirm:

- Codie owns or may use it;
- people shown have given appropriate permission;
- private locations are not exposed;
- the caption is accurate;
- alt text is included.

## Codex rule

Where information is uncertain, Codex must add it to `09-open-questions.md` rather than fill the gap.
