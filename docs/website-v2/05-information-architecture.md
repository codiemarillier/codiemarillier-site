# Information Architecture

## Recommended primary navigation

A strong initial navigation is:

- Home
- About
- Work
- Projects
- Writing
- Investing
- Books
- Travel
- Now

Timeline may be:

- a top-level page;
- part of About;
- or an interactive experience linked from the homepage.

Contact can sit in navigation or as a persistent call to action.

## Homepage

Recommended sequence:

1. Hero
2. Short personal introduction
3. Selected work
4. Current projects
5. Zimbabwe
6. Travel preview
7. Investing and writing preview
8. Books / learning preview
9. Accountability statement
10. Contact invitation

The homepage should not open with portfolio figures.
