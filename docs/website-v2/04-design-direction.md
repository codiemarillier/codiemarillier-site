# Design and Experience Direction

## Inspiration

The website should take strong inspiration from Theo Apteker’s modern, technical and personal presentation.

Do not copy exact layouts, code, animation sequences, visual motifs or written phrases.

Capture the underlying strengths:

- clear personal identity;
- modern technology;
- strong visual hierarchy;
- project-based evidence;
- interactive storytelling;
- personality;
- confidence;
- memorable motion.

## Overall feel

The website should feel:

- dark;
- modern;
- highly interactive;
- technically sophisticated;
- experimental;
- professional;
- personal;
- custom-built.

It must not feel like:

- a generic résumé;
- an investment fund;
- a corporate consultancy;
- a university assignment;
- a standard dashboard template;
- a social-media profile.

## Hero

The hero must use:

# Codie Marillier

Avoid a fixed label beneath the name.

A concise introduction may mention:

- Zimbabwean background;
- current work in England;
- broad interests;
- documentation and accountability.

The final wording should be approved rather than invented.

## Information reveal

The site should begin professionally and reveal personality gradually.

### First impression

- polished;
- ambitious;
- credible;
- modern.

### As the visitor scrolls

- humour;
- travel;
- photography;
- uncertainty;
- mistakes;
- side projects;
- personality.

## Tone

Natural humour is welcome where it fits.

Examples of the intended style:

> My brother and I started our first business when I was eight. It funded tuck-shop money and, eventually, a dog. The exit was less impressive: I went to boarding school.

> I originally moved to France to work on superyachts. Zimbabwe being landlocked eventually became a fairly important consideration.

Humour must not take over the site.

## Colour

Preferred core palette:

- black;
- off-black;
- charcoal;
- white;
- soft grey;
- silver.

Possible restrained accents:

- cool silver;
- ice blue;
- deep forest green;
- muted bronze.

Avoid bright multi-colour gradients unless justified by a specific data visualisation.

## Typography

Use strong, modern typography with clear hierarchy.

Possible characteristics:

- large display type;
- restrained body type;
- technical labels;
- editorial spacing;
- careful line lengths;
- clear contrast.

Do not sacrifice readability for style.

## Motion and interaction

Animation should be an important part of the experience.

Possible uses:

- hero text reveal;
- scroll-driven biography;
- timeline transitions;
- project-card interaction;
- travel map;
- image layering;
- animated financial charts;
- page transitions;
- subtle grid or line movement.

Requirements:

- smooth on mobile;
- no blocking intro;
- no excessive loading;
- accessible keyboard behaviour;
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- maintain readable text;
- no motion solely for decoration if it harms performance.

## Photography

Photography should tell the story.

Potential categories:

- Zimbabwe;
- boarding school;
- cricket;
- family travel;
- France;
- Sweden;
- Cambodia;
- university;
- horsebox work;
- marquee work;
- estate work;
- books;
- current portraits.

Use fewer strong images rather than many weak ones.

Do not publish photographs of third parties without permission.

## Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe should be one of the strongest narrative and visual anchors.

Potential visual references:

- maps;
- coordinates;
- location stamps;
- landscapes;
- family archive photography;
- architecture;
- subtle geographic lines;
- Harare references.

Avoid generic stereotypes or decorative safari imagery disconnected from Codie’s own story.

## Investing visual language

The Investing section may retain a more data-heavy dashboard style.

It should preserve:

- current portfolio;
- performance;
- allocation;
- holdings;
- decision archive;
- journal;
- letters;
- process;
- mistakes;
- benchmarks;
- disclosures.

It must still feel connected to the overall V2 design system.

## Books

The books section should feel like a personal reading library.

Focus on:

> What did this book change?

Possible features:

- currently reading;
- recently completed;
- favourites;
- notes;
- subject filters;
- reading timeline;
- key ideas;
- impact on thinking.

## Travel

Travel should be a main section.

Possible features:

- interactive map;
- featured stories;
- photographs;
- people encountered;
- lessons;
- visit dates;
- region filters.

The section should not feel like a country-count flex.

## Calm versus experimental

The layout can be experimental, but navigation, reading and mobile use should remain simple.

The best result is:

> visually bold, structurally calm.

## V1 and V2 visual integration — 19 July 2026

Codie confirmed that the colours and overall aesthetic of the original investment-led site should carry into Website V2. The V2 structure, large editorial typography, grid, restrained orbit motion and personal storytelling remain intact, but the shared V2 palette now draws from V1:

- deep navy (`#0B1F33`) replaces near-black as the primary dark surface;
- cobalt blue (`#2F5FA7`) becomes the main action and statement colour;
- warm ivory (`#F7F8FA`) and white replace beige as the principal reading surfaces;
- blue-grey (`#E9EEF5`) provides alternate light sections;
- slate text and pale blue details support hierarchy;
- muted gold (`#B08D3A`) is reserved for punctuation, rules, focus and small narrative accents.

V1-style thin borders, clean light cards and restrained editorial shadows now sit inside the more personal and experimental V2 composition. This is a visual integration only: no biography, investment data, journal content, routes or publication status changed.
