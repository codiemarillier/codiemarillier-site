## Competitive sport

Codie appreciates sports that require sustained excellence.

Golf particularly interests him because of the technical difficulty, emotional control and consistency required to remain at the highest level.

Cricket remains the sport most central to his own life.

## Travel

Codie enjoys talking about the people, experiences and environments connected to travel more than simply listing destinations.

Cambodia is his favourite country largely because of the people he met there, especially a guide named Dara.

## Business in Africa

Codie is interested in problems that can become useful commercial opportunities.

Examples include:

- weak infrastructure;
- limited finance;
- inconsistent services;
- fragmented industries;
- property-market inefficiency;
- logistics;
- poor access to trusted providers.

The focus should be on building useful companies rather than romanticising “untapped markets.”

## Physics and the unknown

Codie is fascinated by the parts of physics and reality that humans do not fully understand.

He enjoys thinking about:

- consciousness;
- time;
- the nature of reality;
- quantum physics;
- the structure of the universe.

The website should frame this as curiosity, not expertise.

## Beliefs about work

Codie believes that sustained effort, skill and judgement tend to improve outcomes over time.

He is most motivated by work with:

- visible progress;
- purpose;
- responsibility;
- a clear deadline;
- a strong team;
- a person worth learning from.

## Beliefs about money

Money matters because it can provide:

- time;
- security;
- travel;
- choice;
- family support;
- education;
- freedom from certain daily pressures;
- the ability to invest in ideas;
- more time with people he loves.

Money is not the only measure of a good life, but it can expand the range of choices available.

## Beliefs about ambition

Codie believes ambition is influenced by personality, environment and exposure.

It can be encouraged, but deep ambition is not easily taught.

Ambition is valuable when directed towards things worth achieving.

## Beliefs about risk

Risk should be:

- understood;
- sized;
- survivable;
- reduced where possible;
- judged against the potential upside.

The goal is not maximum risk.

The goal is intelligent risk with favourable outcomes and limited chance of permanent damage.

## Crypto trading failure

Codie made money in cryptocurrency early, became overconfident and later lost much of it through leveraged trading and chasing losses.

This experience shaped his current focus on:

- capital protection;
- written decisions;
- position sizing;
- avoiding leverage;
- distinguishing investment from speculation;
- controlling emotional behaviour.

A suitable public summary is:

> I made money in cryptocurrency early, became overconfident and eventually lost most of it through leveraged trading and chasing losses. It was painful, but it became one of the main reasons I now care so deeply about risk, position sizing and written decisions.

## Happiness

Codie is happiest when spending time with family.

For him, happiness is often belonging and presence rather than constant excitement.

## A good life

A good life involves:

- helping other people;
- continuing to learn;
- improving;
- building useful things;
- sharing experiences;
- maintaining relationships;
- treating people fairly;
- avoiding unnecessary regret.

## Personal principles

### Improve by one percent

Small improvements can compound.

### Never miss twice

A bad day or broken habit should not be repeated the following day.

### Protect sleep

Target roughly 7.5 to 8.5 hours.

### Follow through

Words and actions should match.

### Take regret seriously

The cost of never trying can be greater than a well-considered failure.

## Loyalty

Loyalty becomes visible during difficult periods, not only when relationships are convenient.

The website should value relationships without publishing other people’s private lives.

## Fear of regret

Codie is strongly motivated by the fear of reaching later life and wishing he had attempted more.

This should not justify reckless action.

It supports deliberate action and taking meaningful opportunities seriously.

## Daily routine

Codie is motivated by understanding what his work is improving.
