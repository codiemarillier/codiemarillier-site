# Personal Biography and Background

## Introduction

Codie Marillier is 20 years old, was born and raised in Zimbabwe and is currently living and working in England.

He recently decided to leave university after two years because the environment was no longer helping him build the habits or direction he wanted.

The decision was not simply about leaving education. It was a deliberate attempt to change his environment, work seriously and take greater responsibility for the life he wants to build.

## Zimbabwe and childhood

Codie grew up in Harare and later moved to boarding school in Marondera.

He attended Hellenic and Ruzawi during junior school and Peterhouse for high school.

Boarding school was one of the most important periods of his life.

He loved:

- living closely with friends;
- playing sport every afternoon;
- being part of a disciplined environment;
- having freedom within a strong structure;
- building relationships that lasted for years.

The combination of discipline, sport and friendship strongly shaped his personality.

## Family

Codie’s parents are Zimbabwean and built a real estate company while raising three children.

He often jokes that he was their second-born child because the company was their first.

Growing up around the business gave him early exposure to:

- property management;
- client relationships;
- practical problem-solving;
- entrepreneurship;
- work ethic;
- the sacrifices required to build a company.

Codie is deeply grateful for the life and opportunities his parents created.

That gratitude creates a sense of responsibility: he wants to make full use of the advantages he received and eventually provide a similar quality of life for his own family.

The family property company may be named as **Marillier Properties**, subject to confirmation of the exact public styling and permission to discuss it.

## Sport and cricket

Sport has always been a major part of Codie’s life.

He played cricket, hockey, rugby and tennis, with cricket as the main sport.

His father played cricket for Zimbabwe, leading Codie to joke that he came out of the womb holding a cricket bat.

Cricket taught him:

- preparation;
- teamwork;
- competition;
- resilience;
- discipline;
- the difference between natural ability and sustained effort.

A serious knee injury near the end of high school changed his competitive cricket path.

Cricket should still be prominent on the website because it played a major role in shaping him, but the site must not imply that he is or was a professional player.

## Academic turning point

During his IGCSE O-level preparation, much of the syllabus had been disrupted by online learning.

Shortly before the exams, Codie realised he was behind and spent roughly a month studying intensively, often five to nine extra hours per day.

He compressed large parts of subjects such as biology, accounting and travel and tourism into a short period and ultimately achieved strong results, especially in accounting.

This story demonstrates how he responds when he fully commits under pressure.
