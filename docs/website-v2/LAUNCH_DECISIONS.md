# Website V2 Launch Decisions

Only questions that block photograph publication, preview review or public launch are included here.

### Required before adding photographs

- Which supplied images may be shown in a public preview and on the public website?
- Who owns each image, and does Codie have permission to publish it?
- Who else appears in each image, and is their permission required or confirmed?
- Are the proposed captions, place names and any names of people approved?

### Required before public launch

- What is the final hero copy?
- What are the final permanent URLs, while preserving existing investment URLs and redirects?
- What is the current operating status of O and C Cotswolds Trailers Ltd?
- Which employer and company names may be public?
- What education and qualification wording is verified and approved?
- Should the full portfolio remain public, and what is the update and as-of-date policy?
- Should `/review/` be public, protected or removed from the deployed output?
- Should `/ai/` pages and machine resources be indexable, canonicalised or excluded?
- What is the single main homepage call to action at public launch?

### Safe to defer until after launch

- Dedicated Timeline
- Newsletter
- Contact form
- Downloadable CV
- Broader non-investment Writing index
- Full CMS
- Additional travel destinations
